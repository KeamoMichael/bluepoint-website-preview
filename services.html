01

Financial Advisory & Assurance

Credible financial information. Stronger institutions.

Financial strategy, reporting, revenue, liquidity, assurance and governance brought together to support sustainable delivery.

Long-term financial planning

Driver-based revenue and expenditure forecasts, cash-flow projections and projected financial statements. Scenario and sensitivity analysis tests tariffs, collections, costs, capital expenditure and funding against liquidity, solvency and sustainability indicators.

Municipal finance and budgeting

MTREF assumption reviews, budget funding and affordability, tariff and collection analysis, and SCOA-aligned management information. Connect the IDP, budget, mSCOA information and infrastructure priorities to long-term planning.

GRAP, IFRS and financial reporting

Annual financial statement preparation and review, accounting policies, technical papers, reconciliations and accounting-standard implementation. Includes GRAP 104, impairment and Expected Credit Loss modelling, audit files and misstatement remediation.

Revenue enhancement and financial recovery

Reviews across source data, tariffs, billing, debtors, collection and cash realisation. Includes customer-data cleansing, debtor segmentation, credit control, indigent-management considerations and cost-recovery analysis.

Treasury, liquidity and working capital

Cash forecasting, debtor and creditor analysis, commitments, liquidity gaps, debt affordability and borrowing capacity. Cash optimisation and investment frameworks support timely treasury decisions.

Internal audit, risk and governance

Risk-based, co-sourced and outsourced internal audit; control reviews, performance assessments, pre-audit reviews and verification. Risk registers, early-warning indicators and audit-remediation plans address underlying control weaknesses.

Tax and VAT advisory

VAT reconciliations and recovery, transaction reviews, SARS submissions and queries, audit support and correction of processing errors. Income and provisional tax support, planning, structuring and technical tax matters.

02

Infrastructure, Funding & Economic Development

Affordable projects. Fundable investment.

Connect infrastructure priorities to financial capacity, lifecycle obligations and the requirements of funders and investors.

Financial modelling and investment analysis

Integrated models connect project and operating assumptions to revenue, costs, cash flow and financing. Depending on the assignment, analysis includes debt-service capacity, DSCR, IRR, return on equity, payback and break-even requirements.

Capital raising and funding strategy

Funding strategies across grants, own revenue, debt, development finance and commercial sources. Assess bankability and investor readiness, prepare business cases and support engagement with lenders, funders and strategic partners.

Infrastructure financial planning

Capital investment forecasts, funding options, debt affordability and ongoing operating and maintenance obligations. Lifecycle and replacement costs bring the full cost of asset ownership into investment decisions.

Feasibility and economic analysis

Cost-benefit studies, options analysis, demand and market assessments, commercial viability reviews and resource-mobilisation strategies. Compare financial implications with economic and service-delivery value.

03

Asset Valuation & Investment

Reliable asset information. Better investment decisions.

Integrate accounting, valuation, technical information and lifecycle thinking across institutional assets.

Asset management and GRAP asset accounting

Asset-register development, cleansing and reconciliation; physical verification and condition assessment; useful-life, residual-value and impairment reviews. Accounting support covers additions, disposals, transfers and work in progress.

Infrastructure unbundling and componentisation

Break complex infrastructure networks into appropriate components with distinct useful lives and consumption patterns. Asset hierarchies, replacement costs and component information support depreciation, maintenance and renewal planning.

Property, asset and investment valuation

Market-comparison, income or investment, and cost or replacement-cost approaches, selected for the asset and purpose. Valuation supports reporting, transactions, utilisation, rationalisation and asset investment optimisation.

04

Digital Transformation & Solutions

Less manual effort. More useful information.

Improve financial processes, data quality and management intelligence within the client’s operating environment.

Finance process transformation

Review operating models and workflows across budgeting, reporting, reconciliations, revenue, month-end and asset administration. Redesign processes and automate bottlenecks while preserving controls and governance.

Analytics, dashboards and forecasting

Revenue, debtors, expenditure, cash, assets, budgets and risk indicators brought into trend, variance and exception reporting. Automated forecasting and scenario tools show the financial effect of changing assumptions.

Financial data integrity and systems support

Data cleansing, validation and reconciliation; customer and master-data improvement; system-to-report reconciliations and controls over information flows. Improve traceability and reliability, rather than digitising existing weaknesses.
